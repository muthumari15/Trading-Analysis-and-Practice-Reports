# Trading Analysis and Practice Reports

This repository contains three trading reports based on my paper trading practice and learning experience. The reports cover identifying good-quality trades, understanding and avoiding poor-quality trades, and reviewing focused trading practice. The analysis includes trading strategies, entry and exit decisions, market confirmation, trading mistakes, strengths, weaknesses, and key learnings. The reports also focus on improving entry timing, following the trading plan, using indicators, maintaining discipline, and making better trading decisions through continuous practice and review.
